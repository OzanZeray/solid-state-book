# PLAN: Experimental Data for Introductory Solid State Physics

## Project Overview

A LaTeX book presenting **real experimental data and studies** for every topic found in an introductory solid state physics course (following Kittel's chapter structure). The book does not re-teach theory — it provides the experimental evidence, raw data, and landmark studies that back it up.

**Author:** Ozan Zeray
**Format:** LaTeX `book` class, A4, dark mode
**Source text:** Kittel, *Introduction to Solid State Physics* (chapter/section structure below)

---

## Chapter Structure

Each chapter mirrors Kittel and focuses on gathering/presenting the experimental data behind each topic.

### Ch 1 — Crystal Structure
- Periodic arrays, lattice translation vectors, basis, primitive cell
- 2D and 3D lattice types, index systems for crystal planes
- Simple structures: NaCl, CsCl, hcp, diamond, cubic ZnS
- Direct imaging of atomic structure
- Nonideal structures, random stacking, polytypism
- Crystal structure data tables

### Ch 2 — Wave Diffraction and the Reciprocal Lattice
- Bragg law, scattered wave amplitude, Fourier analysis
- Reciprocal lattice vectors, diffraction conditions, Laue equations
- Brillouin zones
- Reciprocal lattice to sc / bcc / fcc
- Structure factor of bcc and fcc lattices
- Atomic form factor

### Ch 3 — Crystal Binding and Elastic Constants
- Inert gas crystals, van der Waals-London interaction, repulsive interaction
- Equilibrium lattice constants, cohesive energy
- Ionic crystals: Madelung energy, evaluation of Madelung constant
- Covalent crystals, metals, hydrogen bonds
- Atomic and ionic crystal radii
- Elastic strains, stress, compliance/stiffness constants
- Elastic energy density, bulk modulus, compressibility
- Elastic waves in cubic crystals ([100], [110] directions)

### Ch 4 — Phonons I: Crystal Vibrations
- Monatomic basis vibrations, first Brillouin zone
- Group velocity, long wavelength limit
- Force constants from experiment
- Two atoms per primitive basis (optical/acoustic branches)
- Quantization of elastic waves
- Phonon momentum, inelastic scattering by phonons

### Ch 5 — Phonons II: Thermal Properties
- Phonon heat capacity, Planck distribution
- Normal mode enumeration, density of states (1D, 3D)
- Debye model, Debye T^3 law
- Einstein model
- Anharmonic interactions, thermal expansion
- Thermal conductivity, phonon gas resistivity, Umklapp processes

### Ch 6 — Free Electron Fermi Gas
- Energy levels in 1D, Fermi-Dirac distribution
- Free electron gas in 3D
- Electron gas heat capacity, experimental heat capacity of metals
- Heavy fermions
- Electrical conductivity, Ohm's law, experimental resistivity
- Umklapp scattering
- Motion in magnetic fields, Hall effect
- Thermal conductivity of metals, Wiedemann-Franz law

### Ch 7 — Energy Bands
- Nearly free electron model, origin and magnitude of energy gap
- Bloch functions, Kronig-Penney model
- Wave equation in periodic potential, crystal momentum
- Empty lattice approximation, zone boundary solutions
- Number of orbitals in a band
- Metals and insulators

### Ch 8 — Semiconductor Crystals
- Band gap measurements
- Equations of motion, holes, effective mass
- Effective masses in semiconductors (Si, Ge)
- Intrinsic carrier concentration, intrinsic mobility
- Impurity conductivity: donor and acceptor states
- Thermal ionization of donors/acceptors
- Thermoelectric effects
- Semimetals, superlattices, Bloch oscillator, Zener tunneling

### Ch 9 — Fermi Surfaces and Metals
- Reduced/periodic zone schemes
- Construction of Fermi surfaces, nearly free electrons
- Electron/hole/open orbits
- Tight binding, Wigner-Seitz, pseudopotential methods
- Quantization of orbits in magnetic field
- de Haas-van Alphen effect, extremal orbits
- Fermi surface of copper
- Magnetic breakdown

### Ch 10 — Superconductivity
- Occurrence, destruction by magnetic fields
- Meissner effect, heat capacity, energy gap
- Microwave/IR properties, isotope effect
- Thermodynamics of superconducting transition
- London equation, coherence length
- BCS theory, flux quantization, persistent currents
- Type II superconductors, vortex state, Hc1/Hc2
- Single particle tunneling, Josephson effects (dc, ac)
- Macroscopic quantum interference
- High-temperature superconductors

### Ch 11 — Diamagnetism and Paramagnetism
- Langevin diamagnetism equation
- Quantum theory of diamagnetism
- Quantum theory of paramagnetism
- Rare earth ions, Hund rules, iron group ions
- Crystal field splitting, quenching of orbital angular momentum
- Van Vleck paramagnetism
- Isentropic demagnetization, nuclear demagnetization
- Paramagnetic susceptibility of conduction electrons

### Ch 12 — Ferromagnetism and Antiferromagnetism
- Curie point, exchange integral
- Saturation magnetization vs temperature
- Magnons: quantization, thermal excitation
- Neutron magnetic scattering
- Ferrimagnetic order, iron garnets
- Antiferromagnetic order, Neel temperature, AFM magnons
- Ferromagnetic domains: anisotropy energy, domain walls, origin
- Coercivity, hysteresis, single domain particles

### Ch 13 — Magnetic Resonance
- NMR: equations of motion, line width, motional narrowing
- Hyperfine splitting, F centers, donor atoms in Si
- Knight shift, nuclear quadrupole resonance
- Ferromagnetic resonance, shape effects, spin wave resonance
- Antiferromagnetic resonance
- EPR, exchange narrowing, zero-field splitting
- Maser action, lasers

### Ch 14 — Plasmons, Polaritons, and Polarons
- Dielectric function of electron gas
- Plasma optics, dispersion relation, transverse optical modes
- UV transparency of metals, longitudinal plasma oscillations
- Plasmons, electrostatic screening
- Mott metal-insulator transition
- Polaritons, LST relation
- Electron-electron interaction, Fermi liquid
- Electron-phonon interaction: polarons
- Peierls instability

### Ch 15 — Optical Processes and Excitons
- Optical reflectance, Kramers-Kronig relations
- Electronic interband transitions
- Excitons: Frenkel, Mott-Wannier, alkali halides, molecular crystals
- Exciton condensation into electron-hole drops
- Raman effects in crystals
- Electron spectroscopy with X-rays
- Energy loss of fast particles in a solid

### Ch 16 — Dielectrics and Ferroelectrics
- Maxwell equations, polarization, macroscopic electric field
- Depolarization field, local electric field, Lorentz field
- Dielectric constant and polarizability
- Structural phase transitions
- Ferroelectric crystals: classification, displacive transitions
- Soft optical phonons
- Landau theory (2nd order, 1st order transitions)
- Antiferroelectricity, ferroelectric domains, piezoelectricity

### Ch 17 — Surface and Interface Physics
- Reconstruction and relaxation, surface crystallography
- RHEED
- Surface electronic structure, work function, thermionic emission
- Surface states, tangential surface transport
- Magnetoresistance in 2D, IQHE, FQHE
- p-n junctions, rectification, solar cells
- Schottky barrier, heterostructures, semiconductor lasers, LEDs

### Ch 18 — Nanostructures
- Imaging: electron microscopy, optical microscopy, STM, AFM
- 1D electronic structure: subbands, van Hove singularities
- 1D metals, conductance quantization, Landauer formula
- Resonant tunneling, localization, Buttiker-Landauer formalism
- 0D: quantized energy levels, semiconductor nanocrystals, metallic dots
- Coulomb oscillations, Kondo effect, Cooper pairing in dots
- Vibrational/thermal properties of nanostructures

### Ch 19 — Noncrystalline Solids
- Diffraction pattern, monatomic amorphous materials
- Radial distribution function
- Vitreous silica structure, glasses
- Viscosity and hopping rate
- Amorphous ferromagnets, amorphous semiconductors
- Low energy excitations, heat capacity, thermal conductivity
- Fiber optics, Rayleigh attenuation

### Ch 20 — Point Defects
- Lattice vacancies, diffusion (metals)
- Color centers: F centers, other centers in alkali halides

### Ch 21 — Dislocations
- Shear strength of single crystals, slip
- Burgers vectors, stress fields
- Low-angle grain boundaries, dislocation densities
- Dislocation multiplication, strength of alloys
- Crystal growth, whiskers, hardness

### Ch 22 — Alloys
- Substitutional solid solutions, Hume-Rothery rules
- Order-disorder transformation
- Phase diagrams, eutectics
- Transition metal alloys
- Electrical conductivity, Kondo effect

| **[Materials Project API](https://api.materialsproject.org/docs)** | REST API | Same data as above, direct HTTP access. Python client: `mp-api` + `pymatgen` | API key from materialsproject.org |
| **[OPTIMADE API](https://www.optimade.org/)** | Federated REST API | Standardized cross-database queries across Materials Project, AFLOW, COD, ICSD, JARVIS-NIST — crystal structures, composition searches | No key for most providers |

| **[JARVIS (NIST)](https://jarvis.nist.gov/)** | REST API | DFT-computed properties, elastic, optical, solar cell efficiency, 2D materials, phonons | Open access |
