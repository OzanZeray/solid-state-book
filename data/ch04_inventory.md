### Key Historical Context

- **Brockhouse Nobel Prize (1994)** — "for the development of neutron spectroscopy" and "pioneering contributions to the development of neutron scattering techniques for studies of condensed matter"
- The Al dispersion curve (Brockhouse 1955, 1958) is one of the most reproduced experimental plots in solid state physics textbooks
- Woods et al. 1960 (654 citations) — definitive experimental phonon dispersion for a two-atom basis crystal (NaI), showing clear acoustic and optical branches
